Beyond the Cup

The impact behind every cup.

Shanana exists to build a commercially sustainable coffee business that creates opportunity on both sides of the cup — for the people who grow coffee in Rwanda, and for the communities who enjoy it in America.

Our commitments, stated honestly. What follows describes Shanana’s intentions and the outcomes we are working toward. We will report results only when they can be verified — see “Impact reporting” below.

Our four commitments

01

In Rwanda

Women and coffee-growing communities

Women contribute skill and dedication at every stage of coffee — from cultivation to processing. We aim to recognize that work and support women’s economic participation.

  • Support women involved in coffee cultivation and processing
  • Celebrate the skills and contributions of local coffee workers
  • Create economic opportunity within coffee-growing communities
02

In Rwanda

Family income and children’s education

Stable, fair commercial relationships help farming families plan ahead. Our goal is to contribute to steadier incomes — and with them, families’ ability to invest in their children’s education.

  • Help farming families build more stable incomes
  • Contribute to families’ capacity to invest in education
03

In Rwanda

Local employment and agricultural value

Because Shanana is produced and packaged in Rwanda, more of the coffee’s value can be created close to where it grows.

  • Support job creation in production, packaging, logistics and export
  • Promote Rwandan heritage and agricultural products internationally
04

Rwanda & the United States

A lasting economic connection

Our U.S. expansion is designed to create commercial opportunity in America too — through importing, warehousing, logistics, marketing and wholesale distribution.

  • Build long-term relationships with American cafés, retailers and distributors
  • Introduce more consumers to the distinctive character of Rwandan coffee
  • Create a sustainable trade connection between Rwandan producers and American buyers
The journey begins in the mountains of Rwanda. It continues across the ocean — creating possibilities for the people behind every bean.
