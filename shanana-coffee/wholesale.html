How it will work

From origin to your door.

Our planned supply model keeps quality and value creation at origin, with professional logistics in the United States.

Shanana is in its U.S. market-entry phase. Import, regulatory, warehousing and distribution arrangements are being established and are not yet complete. Inventory availability will be confirmed directly with approved buyers.
