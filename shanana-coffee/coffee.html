Our Coffee

Shaped by altitude.
Finished by hand.

Shanana is 100% Arabica from Nyaruguru in Rwanda’s Southern Province — grown high, processed with care, and produced and packaged at origin.

Origin

Nyaruguru, Rwanda.

Our coffee grows in the southern highlands of Rwanda — mountainous terrain where cool air, elevation and volcanic soils give the cherries time to develop slowly and fully.

Country

Terroir

Three conditions. One character.

Great coffee begins long before roasting. In Nyaruguru, the land itself does much of the work.

i.

High altitude

At 1,800–1,900 meters, cherries ripen slowly. That longer maturation contributes to the bright acidity and layered flavors of the cup.

ii.

Cool climate

Cool mountain temperatures support steady, unhurried development — favoring clarity and sweetness over heaviness.

iii.

Volcanic soils

Rich volcanic soils on mountainous terrain nourish the coffee plants throughout the growing season.

Variety

100% Arabica.

Shanana is made exclusively from Arabica coffee — the species prized for its aromatic complexity, sweetness and refined acidity. Grown at altitude, it expresses a clean, elegant profile.

Processing & craftsmanship

Hands-on, step by step.

Every stage of processing involves the skill and attention of local workers. It is careful, human work — and it shows in the cup.

Harvest

Ripe cherries are picked by hand on Nyaruguru’s slopes.

Depulp

The outer fruit is removed to reveal the coffee seeds within.

Ferment

Beans rest and ferment, developing the clarity of the final cup.

Wash

Fresh water carries away remaining fruit for a clean profile.

Dry

Beans are dried with care before being prepared for packaging.

Flavor profile

Layered, balanced, lingering.

Top notes

Citrus & green apple

A bright, vibrant acidity opens the cup with clean, lively fruit.

Heart notes

Honey & florals

Rounded honey sweetness and delicate floral tones at the center of the cup.

Base notes

Smooth & nutty

A subtly nutty finish with balanced complexity and flavors that linger.
