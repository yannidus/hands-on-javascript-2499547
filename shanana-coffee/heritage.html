Our Heritage

Rooted in Rwanda.

Shanana is a Rwandan coffee brand at heart — shaped by the land of Nyaruguru, the skill of the people who tend it, and a pride in sharing Rwanda’s agricultural excellence with the world.

Identity

More than a coffee. A place, and its people.

Every Shanana bag carries the story of where it comes from: the mountains of Rwanda’s Southern Province, the families who cultivate coffee there, and the dedicated hands that harvest, process and package every batch.

We believe heritage is not a decoration. It is the reason the coffee tastes the way it does — and the reason we are building a business that creates opportunity close to where the coffee grows.

The language of our packaging

Traditional storytelling, contemporary form.

Shanana’s matte-black and gold packaging combines illustrated storytelling with modern premium design. Each element carries meaning.

The sun

Sun-like geometric elements speak to warmth, light and the long days that ripen coffee on the hillsides.

The people

Illustrated figures express cultural heritage and human connection — the people behind every harvest.

The bean

Coffee beans and natural decorative details celebrate the richness of Rwanda’s land.

Black & gold

Matte black and a distinctive gold emblem give the story a bold, elegant and contemporary presence.
