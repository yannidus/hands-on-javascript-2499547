Privacy notice

Draft for legal review. This notice is a starting template. Shanana Coffee should confirm the legal entity, contact details and service providers, and have it reviewed by qualified counsel before launch.

Shanana Coffee (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a wholesale inquiry, we collect the details you provide: your name, company, business email, phone number (optional), state and country, business category, product sizes of interest, estimated purchasing volume, sample preference, partnership type and any comments.

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential wholesale or distribution partnership.
  • To arrange samples you request.
  • To keep a record of our business correspondence.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who processes it

Inquiries are delivered through a third-party form service acting on our behalf: Provider to be confirmed. That provider stores submissions under its own security and data-protection terms.

Retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any resulting business relationship, or as required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the information you submitted. Contact: To be published

Changes

We may update this notice as our business develops. The current version will always be posted on this page.
