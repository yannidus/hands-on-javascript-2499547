Chapter II · The Red Bourbon Bean

Grown where the mountains meet the clouds.

In the highlands of Rwanda’s Southern Province, our coffee grows slowly: at 1,800 to 1,900 meters, in a cool climate, on mountainous terrain and volcanic soils.

Cherries are harvested, depulped, fermented, washed and dried with hands-on care from local workers, then produced and packaged in Rwanda.

These hidden layers emerge gradually from careful fermentation and slow drying, a process that draws out the full complexity of the bean rather than rushing it to market.

Aromatic profile

Grown in the high-altitude mountains of Rwanda, our coffee develops a bright acidity and a clean, layered character shaped by the region’s cool climate, volcanic soil, and careful hand processing.
