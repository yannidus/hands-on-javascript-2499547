Our mission

Every neighborhood deserves well-cared-for homes

Too many houses in underserved communities sit vacant or in disrepair, while group home providers struggle to find safe, suitable properties for the people they serve.

We bridge that gap. Akadelices LLC invests in these neighborhoods, brings houses back to life, and partners with responsible care providers so each home becomes a place of stability and support — and a better neighbor on the block.

How it works

From overlooked house to cared-for home

  1. 1

    We buy

    We purchase houses in underserved communities — often as-is, with a simple process and a closing timeline that works for the seller.

  2. 2

    We renovate

    We repair and update each property to be safe, accessible, and comfortable, working with local contractors whenever we can.

  3. 3

    We lease

    We rent the finished home to a qualified group home provider on a long-term lease, so residents have a stable place to live.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of houses do you buy?

Mostly single-family homes in underserved neighborhoods, in any condition. Even if a house needs major repairs, we’d like to hear about it.

Is there any cost or obligation to get an offer?

No. Tell us about the property and we’ll follow up. You’re free to accept or decline any offer.

Who lives in your homes?

We lease our homes to group home providers. The provider operates the home and supports its residents in line with their licensing and local rules.

What do providers need to lease a home?

We work with providers who are properly licensed or certified for their services. Contact us and we’ll walk you through our application.

Can I invest or partner with Akadelices?

We’re always glad to meet contractors, lenders, and partners who share our mission. Choose “Partner / other” in the contact form below.
