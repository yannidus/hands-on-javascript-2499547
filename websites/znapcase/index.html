    FAQ

    Questions? Snap answers.

    How do I get my photo onto the machine?

    Scan the QR code on the screen with your phone and pick a photo — it transfers securely in seconds. Photos are deleted from the machine right after printing.

    Which phones are supported?

    60+ models from Apple, Samsung and Google, with new models added shortly after launch. The machine only shows models that are currently in stock.

    Will the print scratch or fade?

    No. Designs are UV-printed and sealed with a protective top coat that resists scratches, fading and peeling in everyday use.

    Can I use copyrighted images or logos?

    Please only upload images you own or have permission to use. Prints that infringe trademarks or copyright may be declined.

    What if my case comes out wrong?

    Tap “Help” on the machine or scan the support QR code. We'll reprint or refund — no questions asked.
