Barista quality, made automatically

Bean Box grinds, brews and serves café-grade coffee in about a minute. No queue, no staff, no compromise: fresh, fast and customizable drinks for any location, any hour.

[01]

Fresh, every single cup

Whole Rwandan Arabica beans are ground on demand for each drink. Milk stays chilled at 3°C in a sealed module, and every shot is logged for temperature, dose and pressure.

[02]

Fast, without the rush

A 6-axis robotic barista moves with millimetre precision, so a latte takes about 60 seconds, from tap to first sip. Up to 300 cups a day, with no queue.

[03]

Customizable to the last sip

Choose from 30+ drinks, then set the size, milk, strength, syrup, hot or iced. Save your favourite in the app and reorder it with one tap.

[04]

Any location, any hour

Just 1 m² and a standard power outlet. Malls, offices, hotels, campuses and airports: we install, stock and monitor it remotely, 24/7.
