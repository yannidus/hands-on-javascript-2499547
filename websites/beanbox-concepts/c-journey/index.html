Bean Box is a robotic coffee dispenser that makes barista-quality coffee automatically: fresh, fast and customizable drinks for any location. Scroll to follow one cup.

01 — Choose

Tap. Pay. Done.

Pick from 30+ hot and iced drinks on a bright touchscreen, then tap a card or phone. Prefer your own phone? Order ahead in the app and walk straight up.

02 — Grind

Ground for you. Just now.

Whole beans fall from a sealed hopper into a burr grinder, ground fresh for your cup only. Nothing is pre-ground and nothing sits waiting.

03 — Brew

A barista that never rushes.

The robotic arm pulls a 9-bar shot at 92 °C and pours with millimetre precision. Keep scrolling to fill the cup.

0 s~60 s
04 — Make it yours

Your cup, exactly.

Size, milk, strength, syrup, hot or iced. The same perfect recipe every time, and your favourite saved for next time.

Grown in Rwanda.

Every cup starts as specialty Arabica from Rwanda's highland hills: hand-picked, washed at local stations and roasted in small batches. The robot simply finishes what the farmers began.
