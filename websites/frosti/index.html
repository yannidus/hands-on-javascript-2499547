    FAQ

    The scoop on Frosti

    Is the ice cream fresh?

    Yes. Ice cream is made in small batches by our partner creameries and stored at a constant -18°C. Machines are restocked and deep-cleaned on a fixed schedule.

    Do you have vegan or allergen-free options?

    Every Frosti carries at least one plant-based flavour. Full allergen information is shown on-screen for each flavour and topping before you pay.

    How many scoops can I get?

    Up to three scoops per cone or cup, each in a different flavour if you like.

    Which payment methods are accepted?

    Contactless cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and QR payments through the app. Supported mobile-money options vary by country.

    What if my order fails?

    Failed orders are refunded automatically. You can also scan the help QR code on the machine to reach our support team.
