    FAQ

    Good questions, quick answers

    How long does a drink take?

    Most drinks are ready in 45–75 seconds. Iced and multi-shot drinks can take slightly longer.

    Is the coffee really fresh?

    Yes. Whole beans are ground for each cup and milk is kept refrigerated at 2–4°C inside the machine. Every Bean Box is restocked and inspected on a regular schedule.

    Which payment methods are accepted?

    Contactless cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and QR-code payments via the app. Supported mobile-money options vary by country.

    Do you have dairy-free options?

    Oat and almond milk are available at every Bean Box, plus black coffee, Americano and cold brew.

    Something went wrong with my order — what now?

    Scan the QR code on the machine or tap “Help” in the app. Failed orders are refunded automatically, usually within minutes.
