Grown where the mountains meet the clouds.

In the highlands of Rwanda’s Southern Province, coffee grows slowly at 1,800 to 1,900 meters — in a cool climate, on mountainous terrain and volcanic soils. That patience is what you taste in the cup.

From the highlands to your cup

The journey of every bean.

The journey begins in the mountains of Rwanda, where dedicated hands nurture every harvest. It continues across the ocean — bringing remarkable coffee to American communities.

  1. Harvest

    Ripe coffee cherries are hand-harvested on the slopes of Nyaruguru.

  2. Depulp & ferment

    Cherries are depulped and fermented with careful, hands-on attention.

  3. Wash & dry

    Beans are washed and dried by local workers, preserving clarity and sweetness.

  4. Packaged in Rwanda

    Finished coffee is produced and packaged at origin — value created close to the farm.

  5. Export

    Finished, packaged coffee crosses the ocean to the United States.

    Planned

  6. U.S. warehousing

    Inventory held with a qualified third-party warehouse partner.

    Planned

  7. Wholesale partners

    Cafés, retailers, hospitality buyers and regional distributors.
