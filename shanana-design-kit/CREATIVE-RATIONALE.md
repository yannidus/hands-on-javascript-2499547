# Shanana Coffee: Creative Rationale

## The emblem

The Shanana Coffee logo is a bold and elegant emblem that draws from cultural heritage, natural richness, and the warmth of coffee itself. Every element has a meaning, and together they place Shanana as a premium coffee brand with roots in Rwanda.

## Gold on black: excellence and prestige

The use of gold against a black background conveys excellence and prestige, positioning Shanana Coffee as a quality, premium brand.

Gold also reflects the richness and value of coffee as a cherished global commodity. Set against deep black, it gives the emblem a confident, luxurious presence, on the matte-black packaging and everywhere the brand appears.

## Cultural identity and human connection

At the heart of the logo is the profile of a person adorned in traditional attire. It symbolizes heritage, authenticity, and the human hands behind every coffee bean.

This conveys deep respect for the people, traditions, and communities that cultivate coffee.

## The rising-sun motif

The arching lines above the figure suggest a rising sun, a powerful symbol of new beginnings, energy, and the start of the day. It is a natural fit for coffee, the beverage that awakens and energizes.

The motif also hints at the natural environment where coffee is grown: sunlit highlands and fertile lands.

## In summary

| Element | What it communicates |
|---|---|
| Gold on black | Excellence, prestige, a premium brand; the richness and value of coffee as a cherished global commodity |
| Profile of a person in traditional attire | Heritage, authenticity, and the human hands behind every bean; respect for the people, traditions, and communities that grow coffee |
| Arching lines of a rising sun | New beginnings, energy, and the start of the day; the sunlit highlands and fertile lands where coffee grows |

Together these elements express the brand idea: **every sip tells a story.**
